About Kalady Grama Panchayat

Kalady Grama Panchayat is located in Ernakulam district of Kerala. It comes under Angamaly Block Panchayat and Aluva Taluk. Kalady is historically and culturally significant as the birthplace of Adi Shankaracharya, one of India's great philosophers. Situated near the banks of the Periyar River, the region is known for its religious institutions, educational centres, agricultural activities and beautiful natural surroundings. The panchayat represents a combination of traditional Kerala culture, natural resources and modern rural development.

Geographical Information

Understanding the physical characteristics of Kalady

📍 Location

Kalady is situated in Ernakulam district, Kerala. It lies between Angamaly and Perumbavoor and is located close to Cochin International Airport.

🌊 Rivers and Water Resources

The Periyar River is an important geographical feature of the region. It supports agriculture, provides water resources and contributes to the ecological importance of the area.

🌱 Topography

The region is characterised by plains, fertile agricultural lands, low-lying areas and wetlands. These features support different agricultural activities.

🌧️ Climate

Kalady experiences a tropical monsoon climate. The southwest monsoon brings considerable rainfall from June to September. Humid conditions prevail throughout the year.

🌾 Agriculture

Agriculture is an important activity in the region. Paddy cultivation, coconut, banana and other crops contribute to the local agricultural landscape.

🛣️ Transportation

Kalady has road connectivity with Angamaly, Perumbavoor and nearby settlements. Cochin International Airport provides regional connectivity.

Historical and Cultural Importance

Kalady holds a special position in the cultural and spiritual history of India as the birthplace of Adi Shankaracharya. The Sringeri Math, the birthplace memorial and the Sri Ramakrishna Advaita Ashrama are important religious and cultural landmarks. The Periyar River adds natural beauty to the region and has historically influenced settlement and human activities. The combination of cultural heritage, religious tourism and natural resources makes Kalady an important destination in Kerala.
